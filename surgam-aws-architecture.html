Executive Architectural Intent

Surgam Capital powers two lines of mutual fund distribution on one unified foundation: Own B2C clients and B2B Partner MFDs. To eliminate regulatory conflicts, preserve partner relationship trust, and protect investor suitability, the platform mandates four strictly isolated application surfaces backed by an enterprise-grade, event-driven AWS cloud architecture.

The Four Decoupled Application Surfaces
1. Investor Portal & Mobile App B2C & Partner B2B
Users: Own Direct Investors & Partner Clients
  • Calm Self-Service: Portfolio snapshot, XIRR, goal tracking, and upcoming SIPs.
  • Dynamic Co-Branding: Partner clients see the partner's logo, ARN, and EUIN automatically.
  • Trade Approval: Clear summary with tax-impact preview before confirming via OTP.
  • Empower Her: Dedicated female-investor goal spaces and confidential privacy within family accounts.
Security Boundary: Zero visibility across other investors. Tagged by Channel ID.
2. Partner Desk (Web & Mobile) Independent MFDs
Users: Partner Distributors & Sub-Staff
  • Practice Management: Real-time AUM, net sales, live SIP books, and client 360 views.
  • Assisted Initiation: Construct orders and send secure 24-hour approval links to investors.
  • Automated Daily To-Dos: Instant alerts for expiring KYC, failed SIPs, and annual reviews.
  • Payout Drill-down: Full folio-level reconciliation for Model A/B brokerage payouts.
Security Boundary: Row-Level Security (RLS) locks view exclusively to their sub-ARN folios.
3. Operations & Compliance Console Surgam Staff
Users: Ops, Compliance Officers, Principal Officer, Finance
  • Maker-Checker Queues: Segregated approval workflows for master data, payouts, and overrides.
  • Surveillance & AML: Automated alerts for churn switches (<12m) and fast redemptions post-bank change.
  • Feed Reconciliation: Automated matching of daily CAMS/KFintech feeds against exchange trades.
  • Regulatory Complaints: Helpdesk integration with strict 5, 7, and 21-day escalation timers.
Security Boundary: Role-based PII masking. Internal desktop access only.
4. Platform Administration Core Engineering & SecOps
Users: CTO, Platform Engineers, Security Leads
  • Configuration Engine: AMC rate cards, commission models, and scheme cut-off schedules.
  • Consent & DPDP Store: Complete audit log of digital consent, usage purpose, and revocations.
  • 8-Year Audit Explorer: Query access to WORM-compliant immutable ledger for SEBI inspections.
  • Security & Secrets: KMS key rotation, API rate-limiting rules, and feature flag management.
Security Boundary: Hardware FIDO2 MFA + IP Whitelist. No direct financial write capabilities.
Core Principles of Surface Decoupling
Channel-Conflict Guard (CMP-07)
Surgam Relationship Managers (RMs) are cryptographically restricted from querying or viewing clients tagged to Partner MFDs. The system guarantees that partner clients remain strictly owned by the partner.
Hard Suitability Enforcement (CMP-01)
Software blocks orders where a fund's Risk-o-meter exceeds the investor's verified risk score. Overrides require explicit dual-sign-off and recorded investor acknowledgement before reaching the exchange.

The Four Psychological & Regulatory Pillars
1. The Partner Trust Model (The NJ Lesson)
Independent distributors only join a platform if they are certain their client relationships cannot be poached. Surgam enforces cryptographic channel tagging in the database. Even internal relationship managers cannot query partner clients.
2. Software-Enforced Compliance
Human compliance checks break at scale. Surgam automates hard suitability cut-offs, KRA validation, penny-drop bank verification, and AML concentration warnings directly in the pre-trade pipeline before orders are accepted.
3. Friction-Free Operations Automation
A lean operations team handles tens of thousands of monthly transactions. Discrepancies between exchange executions and RTA confirmation feeds are parsed automatically by 11:00 AM daily, surfacing only actionable exceptions.
4. Transparent Payout Engine (PAY-03)
Every rupee of brokerage payout is traceable down to the folio and scheme level. Automated GST invoice matching, TDS deduction, and dispute windows (15th–17th) ensure partner disbursements run with complete trust by the 20th of every month.
